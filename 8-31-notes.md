## Multiplication of Vectors

### Dot Product

- The scalar product of $\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}$ of two vectors $\vec{A} \vec{B}$ is a number defined by the equation 

$$
\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = AB \cos \phi
$$

$$
\vec{A} \cdot \vec{b} = A_x B_x + A_y B_y + A_z B_z
$$

$$
\cos \phi = \frac{\vec{A} \cdot \vec{b}}{AB} = \frac{A_x B_x + A_y B_y + A_z B_z}{AB}
$$

- Multiply magnitudes together then multiply by cos from one vector to the other
  - Make sure you use the angle that is less than 180.

$$
\vec{D} \cdot \vec{C} = 20 \cdot 12 \cdot \cos (157) = -220.92
$$

### Cross Product

- Same as dot but sin instead of cos

$$
\vec{A} \times \vec{B} = 120
$$

$$
\vec{A} \times \vec{F} = 0

$$ 
\vec{D} \times \vec{C} = 20 \cdot 12 \cdot \sin (157) = 93.77
$$
